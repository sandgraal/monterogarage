## What it covers

The reference spans **all generations and markets** of the truck:

- **Generations:** Gen 1, Gen 2, Gen 2.5, Gen 3, and Gen 4.
- **Markets:** United States, Costa Rica, Australia, Europe, the UK, Japan (JDM),
  the Middle East, and global.
- **Engines** across the range — the 4D56 diesel, the 4G54, the 4M40 and 4M41
  diesels, the 6G72, the 6G74 (SOHC, DOHC, and GDI), and the 6G75.
- **Transmissions and transfer cases** — including Easy-Select, Super-Select, and
  Super-Select II 4WD.
- **Trims** from GL, GLS, GLX, and XLS up through Exceed, Super Exceed, Limited,
  and special editions.

Coverage is limited to the Montero / Pajero / Shogun. Closely related Mitsubishis
(Delica, L200/Triton, Raider) are not covered — where a part is shared, you get a
cross-reference note instead of a separate section.

## How to trust what you read

Two labels appear throughout the reference so you always know how solid a claim
is.

### Confidence tiers

Every entry states where its information comes from, on this scale (strongest
first):

1. **Confirmed in the Factory Service Manual (FSM)** — factory documentation.
2. **Technical service bulletin (TSB).**
3. **Community consensus.**
4. **First-hand experience.**
5. **Anecdotal.**

Anything **below TSB** carries a visible caveat, in both languages, telling you to
treat its values and steps as a starting point — not as if they came from the
factory manual.

### Safety notices

Entries about **safety-critical systems** — brakes, steering, suspension, fuel,
airbags/SRS, tires and load ratings, towing, jacking and lifting — carry a
standing **safety notice** in both languages. The site is reference material and
**never a substitute for a qualified mechanic** on this kind of work.

### Sources

Every numeric spec (a torque, a capacity, an interval, a pressure) carries a
citation. Each entry lists its numbered **sources**, with an archived copy link
where one exists and the date the source was read. The Factory Service Manual is
**cited by section, never reproduced** — it is copyrighted.

## Problems

The problem finder is **symptom-first**: start from what the truck is doing.

Search a symptom (_"grinding noise," "hard shifting"_) or browse by symptom.
Every problem page gives you, in order:

- **Can you drive it?** — a plain triage answer up top: _Drive normally · Drive
  gently, repair soon · Do not drive · Tow only._ This appears in **both
  languages at once**, so a reader gets the answer even in the language they don't
  primarily read.
- A **severity** marker — safety-critical, damages other parts, can strand you,
  works worse, or cosmetic.
- **Symptoms**, **diagnostic steps** (what each result points to or rules out),
  **likely causes**, and **fix paths** — each with a difficulty rating and a cost
  band.
- Links to the **parts** and **procedures** involved.
- **First-hand evidence from owners** — real records owners chose to publish and
  link here (shown as their own testimony, never as verified fact).

## Parts

Part numbers for the whole family, done carefully because a wrong number costs
real money and downtime.

- **OEM part numbers**, with how many the vehicle uses and exactly what they fit.
- **Supersession chains** — when a number was replaced by a newer one, you see the
  whole chain, and only the current number is badged **"Order this one."** A
  replaced number is marked, and a chain the data cannot confirm is marked
  **"Verify before ordering"** rather than shown as safe.
- **Aftermarket cross-references** — equivalents worth knowing, each with a plain
  verdict: same maker as the OEM part, reported equivalent, works but wears out
  sooner, or avoid.
- **Where to buy it** — sellers drawn from the community directory. **Nobody pays
  to be listed, and nothing on the page is an affiliate link.**

Part numbers are never guessed. If a number is not in a cited source, it is not
shown.

## Procedures

Step-by-step jobs, each with:

- **Before you start** — prerequisites that must all be true first.
- **Tools** — including any special Mitsubishi tool (SST) numbers.
- **Parts it uses up** — with quantities.
- **Figures this job sets** — torques and clearances, each stored once in the
  reference entry that cites its source and shown here in both languages from that
  single copy.
- **The job, step by step.**
- **What can hurt you on this job** — safety notes.

Every figure links back to the reference entry that cites it, so you can check the
source before you turn the wrench.

## Modifications

Modifications are written up honestly — the tradeoffs are the point, and nothing
here is for sale.

Each mod page tells you:

- **What it needs first** — what has to already be on the truck, or in your hands.
- **What it breaks or affects** — every consequence of fitting it (stops working,
  gets worse, has to be re-adjusted), so you read it **before** you buy, not
  after.
- **The honest tradeoffs**, a difficulty rating, and a cost.
- **Figures this mod states** — each living in its own sourced reference entry.

## Glossary

The bilingual glossary is the backbone of the whole site. It lists the **Costa
Rican terms** the site uses with their **English equivalents**, filterable by
system.

- **Regional variants** from other Spanish-speaking countries (_rin, goma,
  balatas…_) are recorded as searchable **aliases** — you can find a term by any
  variant — but the site's own Spanish text always uses the canonical Costa Rican
  word.
- **False friends** are flagged where a word means something different in Costa
  Rica.

## Community directory

A curated directory of places to find other owners: **forums, Facebook / WhatsApp
/ Telegram groups, Discord servers, owners' clubs, YouTube channels, vendors, and
parts shops.**

Filter by **region, language, generation, and activity level.** Costa Rican and
Spanish-language communities are listed as **first-class entries, not an
appendix.** Each entry says what it is good for and when it was last checked.

The directory is **editorially curated.** Its ordering and inclusion are never
affected by payment — there is no paid placement, paid ranking, or paid inclusion.

## Search and the vehicle filter

- **Search** covers problems, glossary terms, and part numbers at once —
  including the regional variants recorded in the glossary. Search a symptom, a
  term, or a part number (_"brake pads," "transfer case," "MD976075"_).
- **The vehicle filter** — _"Which truck do you have?"_ — lets anyone narrow the
  whole library to one specific truck (generation, market, year, engine, drive).
  Where a match relies on a detail you have not given, the entry is flagged as a
  **provisional match** so you know to confirm it against your own vehicle —
  especially on safety-critical pages. Owners who have filled in a vehicle can
  apply this filter straight from their garage.
