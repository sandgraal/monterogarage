# Roadmap — Coming soon

**Language:** **English** · [[Español|Próximamente]]

Everything on this page is **planned but not yet available.** It is documented
here so you know what is coming — but nothing below works today. For what you can
use right now, see [[For Owners|For-Owners]] and [[For Mechanics|For-Mechanics]].

The features already live today include: no-password sign-in, the vehicle profile
and garage, records and receipts, record attachments, the cover photo, public
showcase and work-log pages, share links with typed scopes and cost-masking,
first-hand community evidence, the mechanic roster with email-addressed link
binding, and the whole reference library.

---

## Shops (not yet available)

Today a "mechanic" is an individual account that holds a share link. The plan
adds **shops as businesses**:

- **Create a shop** and invite other accounts into it as members (invitation
  only — no open join).
- A shared **shop roster**, so a whole shop can see the trucks its members are
  working on — with the owner's consent (see the _"Also let their shop see it"_
  switch, which already exists as a consent control).
- **Claim a shop listing** in the community directory. Claims will be verified,
  and an unverified claim will change nothing a reader sees. Directory ordering
  and inclusion will remain unaffected by whether a shop has an account or a plan.

---

## Propose-and-accept (not yet available)

A carefully bounded way for a mechanic to contribute to a truck's history
**without ever writing into it directly**:

- A mechanic holding the right kind of live link could submit a **proposal** — a
  quote, or a completed job — against the truck.
- A proposal does **nothing** until the **owner accepts it.** If accepted, it
  becomes a record that carries its provenance (who proposed it, under which link,
  and when it was accepted). If rejected, nothing is created.
- An accepted record remains **the owner's own record**, shown as their own
  testimony — never as a site-verified fact.

Until this ships, shared links stay strictly **read-only**: a mechanic can view a
history but cannot add to it.

## Shop monetization (not yet available)

The platform will eventually earn from **shop-side subscriptions** — and this is
bounded by firm rules that will not change:

- **Vehicle owners are free, forever.** No feature that stores, reads, exports, or
  shares an owner's own data will ever be gated by payment.
- **Revoking, expiring, deleting, and exporting your own data are never gated.**
- If a shop's plan lapses, it loses shop **tools** — never its read access to a
  live link a customer gave it.
- **No selling, licensing, or sharing user data** with anyone, ever — no exception
  for aggregated or anonymized data.
- **No paid placement, ranking, or inclusion** in the community directory.
- **No third-party ad or analytics SDKs.**

Planned chargeable shop capabilities are limited to things like roster size beyond
a free allowance, a quote builder, multiple member seats, and branded export of a
history a shop already has a live link on. The site will **not** handle payments
between an owner and a shop — a quote is a document, not a transaction.

---

## Not planned

For clarity, some things are deliberately **out of scope** and are not coming:

- Comments, ratings, or reviews of shops.
- Direct owner-to-mechanic or mechanic-to-mechanic messaging.
- Parts ordering, inventory, scheduling, or dispatch.
- Coverage of other Mitsubishi models beyond the Montero / Pajero / Shogun.
