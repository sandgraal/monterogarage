# For Mechanics

**Language:** **English** · [[Español|Para-mecánicos]]

Montero Garage lets a truck's owner hand you its full service history — safely,
temporarily, and without you signing up for anything. This page explains what you
receive, how to keep it handy, and exactly what you can and cannot see.

## What "mechanic" means here

A **mechanic** is not a separate kind of login. It is simply **someone who holds
a share link on a truck that isn't theirs.** The owner created that link and sent
it to you; it opens their truck's history for you, and for no one else.

You can open a link with **no account at all** — handy when you have a phone in
the bay and a customer waiting. But creating a (free, no-password) account lets
you **keep every shared truck in one place** and find it again without hunting for
the original link.

---

## Opening a link someone sent you

Just open the link. You will see:

- **The truck's identity** — its generation, market, year, and engine.
- **Its history** — the work the owner chose to show you.
- **Costs and receipts** — _only if_ the owner included them (each is a separate
  choice they made).
- **When the link expires and who issued it**, in your language.

No account prompt blocks the content. The page reads the link **in your own
browser** — the link is never sent to a server.

If a link **does not open anything**, it has expired, been ended by the owner, or
was never valid. The page will not tell you which (that would leak information
about the owner's account); just ask the owner for a new one. If the address
looks cut off, the link was probably copied out of a message in pieces — ask the
owner to send it again.

> These shared pages are **private and never indexed by search engines.** They
> exist for the person the link was sent to.

## Binding a link to your account (your roster)

If the owner sent the link **to your email address** and you open it while
**signed in**, you will see a prompt to **Add to my roster**.

Do that once, and the truck appears on your **roster** — _"Trucks shared with
you"_ — every truck any owner has shared with your account, in one place. You no
longer need the original link.

Each truck on your roster shows the date its access ends. The owner can **extend**
that access (even to "until revoked") or **end** it at any time.

If a link was tied to a _different_ account, you can still read it in the browser,
but it cannot be added to your roster.

---

## What you can and cannot see

You see **exactly what the owner chose to share, and nothing more.**

**You can see:**

- The truck's identity and the records the owner opened to you.
- Job costs — **only if** the owner turned that on for your link.
- Receipts — **only if** the owner turned that on for your link (a separate
  choice from costs).

**You cannot:**

- See the owner's other vehicles, their photos, or any record they did not share.
- See costs or receipts the owner kept closed.
- Edit anything, or add anything to the owner's records. A shared link is
  **read-only.**

This is by design: without an account there is no way to write, and even with one,
writing into someone else's truck is not something the platform allows today
(see below).

---

## When access ends

Access ends when:

- the link's **expiry date** passes,
- the owner **ends the link** (revokes it), or
- the owner **deletes** the record or vehicle.

When that happens the truck simply drops off your roster on your next visit.
There is nothing you need to do, and nothing is left behind on your side.

## Coming soon for shops

Today, a mechanic is an individual account holding a link. Features for **shops**
as businesses are on the way but **not yet available**:

- Creating a shop and inviting other mechanics into it as members.
- A shared **shop roster** so a whole shop sees the trucks its members work on.
- Claiming your shop's listing in the community directory.
- A **quote builder** and **propose-and-accept**: sending an owner a proposed job
  or quote that becomes part of their history only when _they_ accept it.

See [[Roadmap|Roadmap]] for the full list. Until those ship, everything on this
page — receiving a link, reading a shared history, and keeping trucks on your
personal roster — already works.
