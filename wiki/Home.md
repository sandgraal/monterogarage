# Montero Garage

**Language:** **English** · [[Español|Inicio]]

Welcome to the user guide for **[Montero Garage](https://monterogarage.com)** — a
bilingual (English / Costa Rican Spanish) platform where any Mitsubishi
**Montero / Pajero / Shogun** owner keeps their truck's whole life in one place:
a named vehicle profile, every receipt, every job — **private by default,
shareable by choice** — on top of a comprehensive reference that covers every
generation and market.

The site was built around one real truck: **Gitana Blanca**, a 2002 Montero.
Every other garage is shaped the same way hers is.

> **Not affiliated with Mitsubishi Motors.** Montero Garage is an independent
> enthusiast site. All reference material is for information only. For
> safety-critical work, consult a qualified mechanic.

---

## Two sides, one site

Montero Garage does two jobs, and you can use either one on its own.

### 1. Your garage (for account holders)

Your **garage** is your private workspace. In it you keep:

- **Your vehicles** — each with a name, its identity (generation, market, year,
  engine), an odometer reading, and photos.
- **Your records** — every job done, receipt, note, and planned job, with dates,
  costs, time spent, and the odometer that day.
- **Your receipts and files** — scans, photos, video, and voice notes attached
  to the record they belong to.

Nobody else can see any of it unless _you_ choose to share or publish it, one
vehicle and one record at a time.

### 2. The reference library (open to everyone, no account needed)

A comprehensive, sourced reference for the whole Montero / Pajero / Shogun
family:

- **Problems** — start from what the truck is doing, get a straight answer on
  whether it is safe to drive, then work through causes and fixes.
- **Parts** — OEM part numbers, the numbers that replaced them, and trustworthy
  aftermarket equivalents.
- **Procedures** — step-by-step jobs with tools, parts, and factory figures.
- **Modifications** — what each mod needs first and what it costs you afterward.
- **Glossary** — the Costa Rican terms the site uses, with English equivalents.
- **Community directory** — forums, groups, clubs, channels, and shops.

You do not need to sign in to read any of the reference.

## A few things that are true everywhere on the site

- **Two languages, equal footing.** Every page exists in English and Costa Rican
  Spanish, at `/en/…` and `/es/…`. Switch language any time; you keep your place.
- **No passwords.** You sign in with a one-time link sent to your email, or with
  Google. There is nothing to choose or forget.
- **Private by default.** Nothing you store is public until you publish it.
- **Your records are your own words.** The site never presents what you wrote as
  a verified fact, and never edits or fact-checks it.
- **Nobody pays to be listed.** There is no paid placement or paid ranking
  anywhere, and nothing on the site is an affiliate link.
